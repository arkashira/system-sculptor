# Business Model Canvas – system-sculptor  

| **Building Block** | **Description** |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Value Proposition** | • **Automated Architecture Insight** – Static and dynamic analysis of codebases to surface hidden coupling, cyclic dependencies, and scalability bottlenecks.<br>• **Actionable Refactoring Guidance** – Generates concrete, prioritized recommendations (module re‑structuring, API contracts, micro‑service boundaries) with impact estimates.<br>• **Continuous Integration Friendly** – Runs as a CI/CD plugin, providing early feedback and trend dashboards to keep architecture debt low.<br>• **Enterprise‑grade Security & Compliance** – Detects architectural patterns that violate SOC‑2, ISO‑27001, and OWASP guidelines.<br>• **Speed & Scale** – Powered by Axentx’s vLLM inference engine and SGLang structured generation, it can analyze millions of lines of code in minutes. |
| **Customer Segments** | 1. **Large‑scale software enterprises** (FinTech, SaaS, IoT) needing to maintain complex, multi‑team codebases.<br>2. **DevOps / Platform engineering teams** that own CI pipelines and want to embed architectural quality gates.<br>3. **Consulting & System‑integration firms** that deliver architecture audits for clients.<br>4. **Regulated industries** (healthcare, finance) where architectural compliance is a legal requirement. |
| **Channels** | • **Direct Sales** – Enterprise account executives targeting C‑level & architecture leads.<br>• **Self‑service Cloud Marketplace** – SaaS offering on major cloud marketplaces (AWS Marketplace, Azure Marketplace).<br>• **Partner Integrations** – Pre‑built plugins for GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, Azure DevOps.<br>• **Developer Community** – Open‑source CLI & VS Code extension to drive adoption and feedback. |
| **Revenue Streams** | 1. **Subscription Licenses** – Tiered SaaS plans (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) billed annually or monthly.<br>2. **Usage‑Based Add‑Ons** – Extra analysis minutes, on‑premise deployment packages, compliance audit packs.<br>3. **Professional Services** – Architecture workshops, custom rule development, integration consulting.<br>4. **Marketplace Revenue Share** – Percentage of sales through cloud marketplaces. |
| **Cost Structure** | • **R&D** – Salaries for architecture analysts, ML engineers, and platform developers.<br>• **Cloud Compute** – vLLM inference clusters, storage for analysis artifacts, CI/CD runners.<br>• **Data Licensing** – Ongoing curation of the 17 M+ code‑pair datasets used for model fine‑tuning.<br>• **Sales & Marketing** – Enterprise sales team, channel partner commissions, developer evangelism.<br>• **Compliance & Security Audits** – Third‑party certifications (SOC‑2, ISO‑27001). |
| **Key Resources** | • **Proprietary ML models** fine‑tuned on Axentx’s massive code‑pair corpus (auto, messages, instr‑resp, query‑resp).<br>• **vLLM & SGLang inference stack** (verified frameworks).<br>• **Domain Knowledge Base** stored in the shared BRAIN (pgvector) for architecture patterns and anti‑patterns.<br>• **CI/CD Plugin SDK** and open‑source CLI/extension codebase (repo: `arkashira/surrogate-1-harvest`).<br>• **Enterprise sales & support team**. |
| **Key Activities** | 1. **Model Training & Continuous Improvement** – Leverage new code‑pair data to keep analysis accuracy high.<br>2. **Product Integration** – Build and maintain plugins for major CI/CD platforms.<br>3. **Compliance Rule Engine** – Update and certify architectural policies for regulated sectors.<br>4. **Customer Success** – On‑boarding, training, and quarterly health‑checks.<br>5. **Community Engagement** – Open‑source contributions, webinars, hackathons. |
| **Key Partners** | • **Cloud Providers** (AWS, Azure, GCP) – for compute credits, marketplace distribution, and compliance certifications.<br>• **CI/CD Platform Vendors** – GitHub, GitLab, Atlassian, Jenkins for deep integration.<br>• **Security & Compliance Auditors** – Third‑party firms that validate our compliance rule sets.<br>• **Academic & Research Labs** – Co‑development of next‑generation architecture‑aware LLMs.<br>• **Axentx Core Teams** – vLLM, SGLang, and BRAIN infrastructure teams for shared resources and data pipelines. |
