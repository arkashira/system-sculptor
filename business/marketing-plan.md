 # marketing-plan.md

## Positioning
1-line: System Sculptor - Streamline software architecture analysis and optimization for maintainable and scalable systems.

Alternatives:
1. ArchitectEase - Simplify software design for faster development and better performance.
2. ScalaFlow - Optimize your code for maximum efficiency and scalability.
3. ArchiBoost - Boost your software architecture with AI-powered insights and recommendations.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
### Persona 1 - John Doe, Senior Software Developer, Budget $500/year
- Name: John Doe
- Daily Job: Lead developer on a team of 5, responsible for designing and maintaining the system architecture.
- Budget: $500/year for tools to improve productivity and scalability.

### Persona 2 - Jane Smith, CTO, Budget $5,000/year
- Name: Jane Smith
- Daily Job: Chief Technology Officer at a mid-sized software company, responsible for overseeing the technology strategy and ensuring the company's software products are scalable and maintainable.
- Budget: $5,000/year for tools to help her team design and optimize software architecture.

### Persona 3 - Alex Johnson, Freelance Developer, Budget $1,000/year
- Name: Alex Johnson
- Daily Job: Freelance software developer, working on various projects with different clients.
- Budget: $1,000/year for tools to help him quickly analyze and optimize software architecture for each project.

## Channels
1. Direct Sales (CAC estimate: $1,500) - Targeting CTOs and senior developers at mid-sized software companies.
2. Content Marketing (CAC estimate: $500) - Creating blog posts, videos, and webinars to educate developers about the importance of software architecture and how System Sculptor can help.
3. Partnerships (CAC estimate: $250) - Partnering with popular developer communities and conferences to reach a wider audience.

## Content Cadence
- Week 1: Launch blog post and video explaining the problem of inefficient software architecture and how System Sculptor solves it.
- Week 2: Webinar featuring a case study of a company that improved their software architecture with System Sculptor.
- Week 3: Blog post and video about the benefits of using System Sculptor for freelance developers.
- Week 4: Webinar featuring a Q&A session with the System Sculptor team.
- Week 5: Blog post about the future of software architecture and how System Sculptor fits into that future.
- Week 6: Webinar featuring a live demo of System Sculptor.
- Week 7: Blog post about the importance of software architecture for startups.
- Week 8: Webinar featuring a panel discussion with industry experts about the role of software architecture in modern software development.

## Launch Milestones
- D-30: Finalize content for the first week, start reaching out to potential partners.
- D-0: Launch the content and start accepting pre-orders.
- D+30: Follow up with pre-orders, gather feedback, and make any necessary adjustments.
- D+90: Evaluate success metrics, adjust marketing strategy as needed, and plan for future content and product updates.

## Success Metrics
- DAU (Daily Active Users): 500 by D+30, 1,000 by D+90.
- $MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) target by D+90: $50,000.
