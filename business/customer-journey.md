 # Customer-Journey.md

## Aware

### Trigger Event
- Developer encounters scalability or maintainability issues in their current project.
- Developer hears about `system-sculptor` through industry forums, blogs, or word-of-mouth.

### Friction Points
- Lack of awareness about the tool and its capabilities.
- Difficulty in understanding the benefits of using `system-sculptor` over existing solutions.

### User Emotions
- Frustration due to project delays and increased workload.
- Curiosity about a potential solution to their problems.

### Opportunities to Delight
- Clear and concise explanations of `system-sculptor`'s capabilities and benefits.
- Success stories and case studies from other developers who have used `system-sculptor`.

### Metric
- Number of developers reached through awareness campaigns.
- Engagement rate on awareness materials (clicks, views, shares).

## Consider

### Trigger Event
- Developer decides to explore `system-sculptor` as a potential solution.

### Friction Points
- Complexity of the tool and its features.
- Lack of detailed documentation and tutorials.

### User Emotions
- Interest in finding a solution to their problems.
- Skepticism about the tool's effectiveness.

### Opportunities to Delight
- Comprehensive documentation and tutorials.
- Free trial or demo of the tool.
- Responsive customer support.

### Metric
- Number of developers visiting the product website.
- Number of trial sign-ups or demo requests.

## Try

### Trigger Event
- Developer decides to try `system-sculptor`.

### Friction Points
- Learning curve associated with using the tool.
- Initial setup and configuration process.

### User Emotions
- Excitement about potentially solving their problems.
- Anxiety about the learning curve and setup process.

### Opportunities to Delight
- Intuitive and user-friendly interface.
- Quick and easy setup process.
- Responsive customer support during the trial period.

### Metric
- Number of trial users.
- Length of trial period for each user.

## Adopt

### Trigger Event
- Developer decides to adopt `system-sculptor` as their primary solution.

### Friction Points
- Cost of the tool.
- Integration with existing development workflows.

### User Emotions
- Relief at finding a solution to their problems.
- Satisfaction with the tool's performance.

### Opportunities to Delight
- Competitive pricing.
- Seamless integration with existing development tools.
- Ongoing support and updates.

### Metric
- Number of paid subscriptions.
- Retention rate of paying customers.

## Expand

### Trigger Event
- Developer expands their use of `system-sculptor` to other projects or teams.

### Friction Points
- Scaling the tool to accommodate larger teams or projects.
- Cost of additional licenses.

### User Emotions
- Confidence in the tool's effectiveness.
- Pride in using a cutting-edge solution.

### Opportunities to Delight
- Discounts for additional licenses.
- Scalability and flexibility of the tool.
- Ongoing training and support for expanding users.

### Metric
- Number of additional licenses sold.
- Growth in usage across teams and projects.
